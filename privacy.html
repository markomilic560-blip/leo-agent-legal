Privacy Policy - Leo Agent

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Leo Agent is a personal automation tool operated by a single individual (Marko Milic). It is not a public-facing application and does not collect, store, or process personal data from any third party or TikTok user.

The only data Leo Agent handles is: (1) video files recorded by its operator, and (2) OAuth access/refresh tokens for the operator's own YouTube, Instagram, and TikTok accounts, used solely to publish the operator's own videos to the operator's own accounts.

No data is shared with, sold to, or accessible by any third party. All tokens and credentials are stored locally and privately by the operator.

For questions or data deletion requests, contact: marko.milic560@outlook.com
